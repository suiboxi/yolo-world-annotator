# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from feedback.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, sexualized attention, threats, sustained disruption, and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code. They may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or another private channel the maintainers publish. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be removed from the project team.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
